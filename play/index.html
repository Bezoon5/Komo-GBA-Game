The story

A giant in black armour killed Pieter de Vries, the finest trader the Dutch company ever sent east. His daughter Lieve, red-haired and forbidden to set foot in Japan, follows the killer up a five-storey pagoda. Each floor is held by a master of a different fighting style.

Bow, spare, or show no mercy: honour decides who helps her, who comes back for revenge, and how it ends.
