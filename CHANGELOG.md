# Changelog

## v5.5.1
- Fixed speckled garbage around the fighters in accurate emulators (mGBA, including the web player) and on hardware: sprite tiles are now cleared from a zero kept in RAM, because a DMA reading the cartridge can't hold its source address fixed.

## v5.5
- Weighted motion for guards, sidesteps, the backstep, hit reactions, staggers and deaths.
- The hakama trails the direction of travel and swings after each leg.
- Each stage's light colours the fighters; lightning whitens them after the flash.
- Sword binds: when two cuts meet, tap A to push; the loser is thrown open.
- Hit-stop and screen shake depend on the weapon.
- Cutscene portraits have four expressions: calm, angry, hurt, respectful.
- A slow-motion replay of the final cut.
- A spared master returns on the way to the roof: to help (high honour) or for revenge (low honour).
- A challenge on every floor, saved once met.
- A new ending for a climb without honour.
- The story (honour, spared masters, challenges) is saved at each floor; the pause menu offers Save and Quit.

## v5.4
- Realistic motion for the walk and the cuts: weight, lean, follow-through.
- Painted shading: blended tones, contact shadows, coloured edges instead of black ink.

## v5.3
- Fixed parts of fighters disappearing in the spare and iai close-ups (the GBA's per-line sprite limit).
- Fixed wrong colours below the black-and-white defeat wipe.
- The iai's leaf now falls into a red reticule.
- Cut or guard in the middle of a sidestep, jump, dodge or dash; guard while edging in or out.

## v5.2
- Character redesigns from the designer (Kenji, Hōzen, Kagerō, Kurogane, the sailor, Morsel).

## v5.1
- L and R step through seven planes; Up jumps; Down crouches.
- Sparing and the iai can no longer be skipped by mashing A.
- The defeat fades to black and white down the screen, with the death poem over the still scene.

## v5.0
- The pier prologue and the secret spire; gauntlet and time attack.
- Disarms, stage hazards, the iai showdown, honour and sparing.
- Reflections, the chiburi and nōtō, the Lantern Hall spotlight, clothing cuts, dawn, death poems.

## v4.x
- Lightning silhouettes, living stages, woodblock stills, portraits, ink transitions, master intros, the killing-blow zoom, the growing storm, ring-outs, the trophy finale.

## v1–v3
- From the first prototype to five floors with cutscenes, a soundtrack, timed parries and height-matched blocking.
